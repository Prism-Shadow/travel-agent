# The open milestones (M3, M5) are withdrawn from the plan

The README's milestone table planned two things that had not happened yet: M3, the
one-sentence-to-payment-page acceptance run, and M5, flights. Both rows are removed from
both READMEs — what this product does next is deliberately unplanned until a
product-direction discussion settles it. Delivered milestones (M0–M2, M4) stay as the
record of what exists.

design/001 §8 keeps its milestone table and dependency graph as a historical record, with
a dated note that the M3/M5 rows are no longer commitments; the doc's status header says
the same. design/004 (the browser-workspace roadmap) is untouched: its remaining open
items were already gated on the release-path decision, which is part of the same future
discussion.
